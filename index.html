Bring a yoga mat, a water bottle, and a towel. Comfortable clothing that allows for a full range of motion is also recommended. Some studios provide mats and other props, but it is always a good idea to check in advance.
No prior experience is required. Our beginner-friendly classes include clear instructions and modifications for different levels of flexibility and fitness.
We offer beginner yoga, Hatha yoga, Vinyasa flow, restorative yoga, mobility-focused sessions, and guided meditation classes.
We recommend arriving 10 to 15 minutes before the class begins. This gives you enough time to check in, prepare your mat, and settle into the space.
Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before participating, and inform the instructor before class. The instructor may suggest safer modifications or recommend a more suitable session.
